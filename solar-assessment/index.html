Questions

Before you enquire

How many panels do I need?

It depends on how much power you use, when you use it, and your roof. We size the system from your bills and household habits rather than filling the roof by default.

Should I get a battery now or later?

For some homes a battery makes sense from day one; for others it’s better to start with solar and add a battery later. We’ll show you both options with the numbers, and you decide.

Which rebates and incentives apply?

The price range already includes federal incentives. You may also be eligible for a Solar Victoria rebate; we check what applies to your home and show it clearly in your quote.

What happens after I submit the form?

An engineer will call you within one business day. To give you an accurate quote we’ll ask for a recent electricity bill and a couple of photos of your roof and switchboard.

Is there any obligation?

None. The assessment is free, and there’s no pressure to go ahead.
