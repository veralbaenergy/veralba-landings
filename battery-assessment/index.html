Your numbers first

Where a battery earns its keep

During the day your panels make more than you use. A battery stores that extra and hands it back in the evening, when your household use peaks and grid power costs the most. How big it should be depends on your evenings.

Sometimes the numbers say a battery won’t pay for itself yet. When that’s the case, we’ll tell you.

Questions

Before you enquire

Can I add a battery to my existing solar system?

In most cases, yes. Most battery retrofits come with their own hybrid inverter, which works alongside your existing solar system. We check your current inverter, your switchboard and whether your home is single or three phase, and explain the options in your assessment.

How do you work out the right battery size?

We look at your electricity bills, how much solar you currently export and how much power you use in the evening and overnight. The goal is a battery that pays its way, not the biggest one we can sell.

Does the price include backup during outages?

No. The range above is without backup. If keeping the lights on during an outage matters to you, tell us and we’ll quote partial or whole-home backup separately.

Which rebates and incentives apply?

The price range already includes the federal battery discount. Other incentives depend on your eligibility, so we check what applies to your home and show it clearly in your quote.

What happens after I submit the form?

An engineer will call you within one business day. To give you an accurate quote we’ll ask for a recent electricity bill and a few photos: your switchboard, your inverter label and where the battery could go.

Is there any obligation?

None. The assessment is free, and there’s no pressure to go ahead.
